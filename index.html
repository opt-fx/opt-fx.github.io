OPT-FX

Free browser apps for samplers and synths, old and new. Nothing to download: open one in Chrome or Edge and plug in.

Support OPT-FX on Ko-fi Request an app

How they work

Each app is a single web page that runs on your own computer. Your sounds and patches stay with you; nothing is uploaded. The apps that talk to hardware use Web MIDI or Web Serial, which Chrome and Edge support. Apps marked installable can be added to your Dock or Start menu, open in their own window and work offline.

Feedback and support

Every app has a GitHub page with its README and an issues list for bug reports and requests. The apps are free to use. If they've earned a place in your studio, you can buy OPT-FX a coffee on Ko-fi.

Requests and feedback

Ideas for a new app, a feature you'd like, something that isn't working, or just how you're using them: it all comes straight to OPT-FX.
